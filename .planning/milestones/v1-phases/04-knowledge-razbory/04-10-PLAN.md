<objective>
Close gap G-04-2: not-yet-ready (announcement) разборы must not expose a read CTA.

When a chronology row's `item.status === 'announcement'`, `ChronologyItem` must suppress
the `<Link>Читать разбор →</Link>` and instead render the honest placeholder text
«Разбор этой темы ещё готовится — следите за обновлениями». Published rows are unchanged.

Purpose: Visual honesty (RAZB-01) — a razbor that is not published yet is not readable, so
a "read" affordance is misleading. UAT test #2 reported this as a major issue.
Output: A thin presentational conditional in `ChronologyItem.jsx` plus Playwright coverage.
</objective>
