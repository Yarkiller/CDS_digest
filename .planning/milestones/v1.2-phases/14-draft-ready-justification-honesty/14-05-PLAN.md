## Root causes

- G-14-2a: the row meta flex container (~759-781) renders the raw `{item.material_status}` pill (`draft`/`ready`) and the decision caption (`«одобрен»`/`«отклонён»`) side by side with no disambiguation. `material_status` and `decision` are independent fields (D-02: approve ≠ ready), so an approved draft legitimately shows `draft` + `одобрен`, which reads as a contradiction.
- G-14-2b: the sticky footer (~806-834) renders an extra `<ul>` of `approvedDrafts` titles (a strict subset already rendered in the shortlist list), and the batch CTA label «Сделать ready одобренные черновики» is a bare noun phrase with no count.
