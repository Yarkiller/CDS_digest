# Phase 9 Plan 04 — Composition wiring + settings + CAP-02 deferred proof

## Objective

Wire the Supabase service-role client and `SupabaseDraftPersister` into the composition root, load the new environment settings, and prove two cross-cutting behaviors using the in-memory fakes: idempotency/overflow of the persist use-case, and the Phase 7 deferred CAP-02 proof that a captions failure writes zero rows through the persist port.

Purpose: Make the persist path runnable from composition and verify the fail-closed envelope before Phase 10 builds the Typer CLI.
Output: Updated `Settings`, `clients.py`, `.env.example`, and the final unit proofs for idempotency, overflow, and zero-row captions failure.
Scope note: 4 tasks stay together as the close-out wave. Settings/clients are the composition contract Phase 10 will import; overflow and CAP-02 proofs close PERS-02 and the Phase 7 deferral against the same fake port. Splitting would add a plan without reducing coupling.
