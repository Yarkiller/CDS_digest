# Phase 18 Plan 03 — Tracer: the email-preview CTA escapes the sandbox, and the shortlist link reaches the preview surface (ADUX-08)

<objective>
Lead this plan with one production-quality end-to-end slice through every remaining layer the phase touches: the backend email renderer (`render_material_email_block`'s anchor attributes) → the SPA mock renderer that mirrors it (`composeMockPreviewHtml`) → the SPA component that hosts the sandboxed iframe (`AdminDigestPage.jsx`) → the Playwright proof that the CTA now opens a top-level context, plus the unit proof that the anchor shape changed while the href did not.

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 4 asks that the admin shortlist and email-preview links reach the admin preview surface and that the email-preview CTA navigate the top-level app rather than being trapped in the `sandbox=\"\"` iframe. D-11 satisfies that requirement **behaviourally** and narrowly: the shared href builder `render_email_html` stays `{base}/materials/{slug}` for both the preview and the real send (so no admin URL ever reaches ordinary readers' mail and `test_preview_email_html_matches_send_html` stays valid), the anchor gains `target`/`rel`, and the iframe gains `allow-popups allow-popups-to-escape-sandbox` with `allow-scripts`/`allow-same-origin` deliberately left off. Only the shortlist link is repointed (D-10) — that is where backlog 999.1's draft-404 actually lives.

Output: the anchor attributes in `email_render.py` and their mock mirror in `adminApi.js`, the widened iframe sandbox and repointed shortlist link in `AdminDigestPage.jsx`, added assertions in `tests/unit/test_email_render.py`, and the new escape spec plus 4 amended assertions in `tests/admin.spec.js`.
</objective>
