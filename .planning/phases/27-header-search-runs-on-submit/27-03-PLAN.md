<objective>
Widen the proven tracer to the two behaviours that share the trigger path: results must come back with zero requests (cache across remount / Back / return from a material) and a deliberate re-submit of the same query must still hit the network exactly once (the one-shot `submitId` marker in navigation state), with X-2 installed to prove the restore path shows no in-flight affordance.

Purpose: C-04, C-09, C-10 and C-11 are all consequences of the same design question — what identifies "the search we already ran". The answer is two distinct identities: `(role, q)` for the cache and a `submitId` for a forced re-run. Building them together keeps the identity model in one place instead of discovering later that the cache swallows the re-submit.

Output: the module-scope snapshot cache with `lastRoleForQuery`, the `submitId` navigation protocol in `SearchPill.jsx` plus its consume-and-strip side in `KnowledgePage.jsx`, and the restore-silence assertions built on 27-02's harness-side X-2 log.
</objective>
