## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |

## Success Criteria

- A fresh video produces a new `status=draft` material plus a pending shortlist item, with `already_saved: false`.
- The rendered `##` list follows the talk and contains none of the six legacy scaffold headings.
- Every `## Конкретика` bullet is traced to the transcript, or the section is honestly absent with the reasoning recorded.
- `## Зачем это СВА` is present when an analog exists and honest about its limits, or honestly omitted; `dek` stays a 1-2 sentence hook.
- `response_chars` is recorded against the 16000 escalation band.
- The owner has read the draft and either approved it or named a defect.
